Prevent Health Problems: Understand Your Risks

High Chances of Asthma

Asthma

Risk of asthma symptoms is High when AQI is severe.

Do's
  • Stay indoors
  • Use air purifier
  • Wear mask
Don'ts
  • Avoid outdoor exercise
  • Do not smoke

High Heart Risk

Heart Issues

Air pollution increases risk of heart attacks and stroke.

Do's
  • Stay indoors
  • Use air purifier
  • Keep medications ready
Don'ts
  • Avoid heavy exercise
  • Avoid polluted areas

High Allergy Risk

Allergies

Pollution can trigger sneezing, irritation, and skin allergies.

Do's
  • Wear mask outdoors
  • Keep windows closed
  • Use air purifier
Don'ts
  • Avoid dust exposure
  • Do not ignore symptoms
